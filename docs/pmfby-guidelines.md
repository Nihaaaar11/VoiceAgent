# PMFBY Operational Guidelines Core Reference

## 1. Statutory Premium Limits (Paid by Farmer)
- Kharif Food & Oilseeds: 2.0% of Sum Insured
- Rabi Food & Oilseeds: 1.5% of Sum Insured
- Annual Commercial / Horticultural: 5.0% of Sum Insured

## 2. Mandatory Loss Reporting Timelines
- Localized Perils (Hailstorm, Landslide, Inundation, Cloudburst): Strictly within 72 hours via Crop Insurance App, Toll-Free Helpline, or Bank.
- Post-Harvest Drying Losses: Up to 14 days maximum from harvest while crop is drying in cut-and-spread condition in the field.

## 3. Reduction / Deduction Clauses
- Section 25 (Area Correction Factor - ACF): Proportional reduction applied across an entire village/block if total insured acreage exceeds the government-notified sown acreage.
- Section 21.2 (Actual vs Threshold Yield): Shortfall ratio calculated using village-level Crop Cutting Experiment (CCE) Form-2 trial averages, not single-farm damage.
- Section 21.3.5 (Prevented Sowing): Lump-sum payout capped at 25% of Sum Insured if >75% of the block fails to sow.
